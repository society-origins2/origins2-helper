Das lässt sich beim Betrieb einer Website nicht vermeiden und dient der Auslieferung und der Abwehr von Angriffen. Rechtsgrundlage ist unser berechtigtes Interesse am sicheren Betrieb (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO). Wir selbst haben auf diese Protokolle keinen Zugriff. Hinweise von GitHub: docs.github.com/privacy

Keine Cookies, kein Tracking

Diese Seite setzt keine Cookies und verfolgt dich nicht über andere Seiten hinweg. Deshalb gibt es auch kein Cookie-Banner.

Was du einstellst, bleibt in deinem Browser: Auswahl im Rechner, eingetragene Marktpreise, abgehakte Materialien, Sprache, Weihnachtsmodus, Boss-Erinnerungen. Das liegt im lokalen Speicher deines Browsers (localStorage) und wird nicht an uns übertragen. Du kannst es jederzeit über die Einstellungen deines Browsers löschen.

Schriftarten

Die Schriften Cinzel und Inter liefern wir selbst von dieser Seite aus. Es wird keine Verbindung zu Google Fonts oder einem anderen fremden Server aufgebaut.

Besucherzähler

Sofern der Zähler eingeschaltet ist, nutzen wir GoatCounter. Der Dienst zählt Seitenaufrufe ohne Cookies und speichert nach eigenen Angaben keine personenbezogenen Daten und keine IP-Adressen. Wir sehen damit nur, wie oft die Seite aufgerufen wurde, nicht von wem. Rechtsgrundlage: berechtigtes Interesse an einer einfachen Reichweitenmessung (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO). Weitere Hinweise: goatcounter.com/help/privacy

Privacy

Origins2 Helper by Society · Last updated: 25 November 2026

The Origins2 Helper is a private, voluntary fan project by the guild Society. It is not part of Origins2, it is not published by the server team and it does not make money. There are no ads, no accounts and no paid features.

Who is responsible?

Guild Society, represented by xZcRiTiCaLZz.
The quickest way to reach us is Discord: xZcRiTiCaLZz on Discord – or the “Report bug / suggest idea” button on the site.

What happens when you open the site

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you open it, GitHub processes technically necessary connection data, including your IP address, the time of the request and the file requested. This cannot be avoided when running a website and serves delivery and protection against attacks. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We have no access to these logs ourselves. GitHub's notes: docs.github.com/privacy

No cookies, no tracking

This site sets no cookies and does not follow you across other sites. That is also why there is no cookie banner.

Whatever you set stays in your browser: calculator selections, market prices you enter, ticked materials, language, Christmas mode, boss reminders. All of it lives in your browser's local storage and is never sent to us. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Fonts

The fonts Cinzel and Inter are served from this site itself. No connection is made to Google Fonts or any other third-party server.

Visitor counter

If the counter is switched on, we use GoatCounter. The service counts page views without cookies and, by its own account, stores no personal data and no IP addresses. All we see is how often the page was opened, not by whom. Legal basis: legitimate interest in simple reach measurement (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). More: goatcounter.com/help/privacy

Reporting bugs, suggesting ideas, joining as a helper

These forms are voluntary. What you fill in is sent to a channel on our Discord server (Discord Inc., USA). Only what you enter is transmitted:

  • for reports: the area, your description and, if you attach one, your screenshot
  • for helper or promoter applications: in-game name, Discord name, optional channel link, promoter code and character screenshot

Technically your browser connects to Discord for this, which transmits your IP address to Discord. The legal basis is your consent by sending the form (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Please don't enter anything that is nobody else's business – we don't need real names, addresses or account details. Messages stay in our Discord until we delete them there.

Chat “Ask xZcRiTiCaLZz”

The chat searches this site's knowledge base. That search runs entirely in your browser; your question does not leave your device. The only thing stored in your browser is how many questions you asked recently, as a brake against spamming.

Once AI answers are switched on, your question is passed, together with the matching knowledge base entries, to a service at Cloudflare and from there to an AI provider. This transmits your IP address to Cloudflare. The chat window says so. The legal basis is your consent by sending the question (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). We do not store the questions.

Links to other sites

We link to Origins2, Discord, Kick and similar sites. The respective providers are responsible for their content and how they handle data. Nothing from there is embedded here – you only leave this site when you click a link.
